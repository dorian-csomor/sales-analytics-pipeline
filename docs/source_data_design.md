# Source data design

The source database simulates the operational system of **Vireo Wholesale**, a fictional
distributor of office and facility supplies. It sells to about 500 business customers in five
countries (Hungary, Austria, Czechia, Slovakia, Romania). The data covers **January 2023 to August 2026**.

The data is generated by `scripts/generate_source_data.py` with a fixed random seed, so it is
fully reproducible.

## Tables (schema `ops`)

| Table | Rows (approx.) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `sales_reps` | 14 | Account managers, one territory (country) each |
| `customers` | 535 | Customer master: segment, city, country, current account owner |
| `products` | 96 | Product catalogue: 5 categories, 10 suppliers, current list price in EUR |
| `product_cost_history` | 350 | Purchase cost per product with validity periods |
| `fx_rates` | 176 | Monthly average rates: HUF, CZK, RON (and EUR) per 1 EUR |
| `orders` | 18,500 | Order headers, amounts in local currency |
| `order_lines` | 62,000 | Products, quantities, prices and discounts per order |
| `returns` | 1,150 | Returned quantities with reason |

## Business patterns built into the data

These are what the management report should surface:

1. **Growth stalled in 2025.** Revenue grew about 15% in 2024 and only about 1% in 2025.
2. **Romania is the main reason.** The key account manager left at the end of March 2025. Their customers
   were handed to a newer colleague, and Romanian revenue dropped by about 25% in 2025 due to lost
   customers and lower order frequency.
3. **Tech Accessories: growing sales, shrinking margin.** It's the fastest-growing category, but supplier cost
   rises (March 2025 and January 2026) weren't passed on to customers. Gross margin fell from about 30% to about 15%.
4. **One supplier has a quality problem.** Products from *Aurora Hygiene* are returned about 6.5% of the time,
   against about 1–3% for other suppliers. Most returns are marked "Defective".
5. **Seasonality.** Demand dips in July–August and peaks in September–November.
6. **Segments behave differently.** Enterprise customers order often, in large quantities and at higher discounts. SMB
   customers order rarely and churn more.

## Data quality issues built into the data

Every one of these must be detected and handled by the ETL pipeline:

| # | Issue | Where | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Order date stored as text in 5 formats (`2025-03-14T10:22:05`, `2025-03-14`, `2025.03.14.`, `14.03.2025`, `14/03/2025`) | `orders.order_date` | all rows |
| 2 | Impossible years from typos (`2052` instead of `2025`) | `orders.order_date` | 6 orders |
| 3 | Order entry timestamp (`created_at`) differs from the business order date for manually entered orders | `orders` | ~60% of manual orders |
| 4 | Duplicate orders (web shop double-submit): same `order_number`, new `order_id` | `orders`, `order_lines` | 67 orders |
| 5 | Status spelled inconsistently (`Delivered`, `delivered`, `DELIVERED`, `Delivered `, `Canceled`, …) | `orders.status` | ~8% |
| 6 | Currency codes messy (`huf`, `Ft`, NULL) | `orders.currency` | ~3% |
| 7 | Hungarian orders labelled `EUR` although the prices are in HUF (about 400× too high) | `orders.currency` | 15 orders |
| 8 | Missing unit price | `order_lines.unit_price` | ~0.8% |
| 9 | Discount stored as a whole percentage (`10`) instead of a fraction (`0.10`) | `order_lines.discount_pct` | ~550 lines |
| 10 | Zero or negative quantities | `order_lines.quantity` | ~185 lines |
| 11 | Duplicate products from an ERP migration (same item, different SKU spelling, no cost history) | `products` | 8 products, ~1,900 lines |
| 12 | Duplicate customers (same company, name spelled differently) | `customers` | 6 customers |
| 13 | Country typed in many ways (`HU`, `Magyarország`, `Czech Republic`, `Österreich`, …) or missing | `customers.country` | ~17% |
| 14 | Customer names with stray spaces, all-caps, or missing punctuation | `customers.customer_name` | ~5% |
| 15 | Test / demo customers and their orders | `customers`, `orders` | 2 customers, 40 orders |
| 16 | Orphan orders: customer no longer exists in the master | `orders.customer_id` | 10 orders |
| 17 | Delivered orders without a ship date | `orders.ship_date` | ~180 orders |
| 18 | Amounts in local currency (HUF, CZK, RON, EUR); product cost must be matched by date (as-of join) | `order_lines`, `product_cost_history` | by design |
